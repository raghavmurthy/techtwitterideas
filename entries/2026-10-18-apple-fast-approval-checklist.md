# Checklist for fast App Store approval

**X post:** https://x.com/MaxHirsch13/status/2111671023615852786  
**Author:** max (@MaxHirsch13) · posted 2026-10-18 (UTC) · 210 likes, 12 replies  

## What it is
@MaxHirsch13 lists what helps an app get approved by Apple quickly: no review prompts during onboarding, a straightforward paywall with the upfront price in the larger font, support for all iPhone models, no UI/UX bugs, and a demo account for the reviewer. Replies are mixed: one says the paywall-font point is real, others report slow reviews regardless, and one clarifies that iPhone-only apps only need to work in iPad's fixed iPhone-size window.

## Thread highlights
> **@arnavsawantt:** ours just got approved and the paywall font thing is real. apple cares more about that than half your features

> **@bytwize:** right. if the app targets iPhone only, iPad shows it in a fixed iPhone-size window, so filling the screen isn't expected. it just has to work in that window: nothing cut off, no crashes, login works

> **@jaequbh:** yeah right lmao every step takes 2 weeks to a month for response

## Implications
- Mobile games with subscriptions hit the same review points, especially paywall price presentation and reviewer access.
- **Experiment:** before the next iOS submission, audit onboarding and paywall against these five points and record review turnaround.
