# Mixar: a team of AI agents that model, texture and light a 3D scene

## What it is
@NBhargava18 launched Mixar (@mixie3D): you describe a scene and multiple agents model, texture and light it in parallel, and you can step in and change anything. The launch film was made in the tool.

## Implications
- Parallel agents per discipline (model/texture/light) is a useful structure to compare with single-agent Blender MCP loops for environment blockouts.
- **Experiment:** block out one small scene in Mixar and check whether the export is usable low-poly in a game engine.
