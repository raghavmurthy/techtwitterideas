# Google Play lets you install private vibe-coded apps

**X post:** https://x.com/ssamat/status/2110073098380644709  
**Author:** Sameer Samat (@ssamat) · posted 2026-10-13 (UTC) · 3334 likes, 133 replies  

## What it is
Sameer Samat (@ssamat), President of the Android Ecosystem at Google per his bio, announced Play Store changes: wirelessly install and run your own vibe-coded apps privately on your device, discovery via Ask Play and Gemini, and new Game Finder and Gamer DNA discovery features. @teja2495 adds that no developer account is needed, only a regular Google account, apps stay out of the public catalog, and sharing with friends and family is planned.

## Thread highlights
> **@teja2495:** You don't need a developer account, just a regular Google account is enough. These apps stay private and won’t appear in the regular Play Store catalog.

## Implications
- Private installs lower friction for testing game prototypes on real Android hardware with non-developer testers.
- Game Finder and Gamer DNA may change how games are discovered on Play.
- **Experiment:** once available, install a prototype via the private path and compare setup time with internal testing tracks.
