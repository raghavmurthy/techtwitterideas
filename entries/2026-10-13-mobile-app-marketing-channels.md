# An indie developer's ranking of mobile app marketing channels

**X post:** https://x.com/carlmonkft/status/2110068741530673524  
**Author:** Open Book (@carlmonkft) · posted 2026-10-13 (UTC) · 975 likes, 53 replies  

## What it is
@carlmonkft, who says he focuses on TikTok ads and UGC, ranks channels for mobile apps: TikTok and Meta ads (strong once creatives and user economics work), 'Canvas UGC' on fresh accounts, TikTok slideshow farms and organic slideshows (highest ROI, TikTok only), Reddit, X, organic and paid UGC, ASO (amplifies everything), clipping, YouTube Shorts, AI indexing (underrated, since AI recommendations read as advice), affiliates, SEO, Apple Search Ads (mostly profitable only on Apple's free credit), influencers (audience saturation), Pinterest, Snapchat, email (only for re-engagement), and Product Hunt (which he calls useless). Some tactics he mentions, like buying upvotes and phone farms, break platform rules.
