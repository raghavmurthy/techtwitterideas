# Don't report instantly cancelled trials to Meta

**X post:** https://x.com/cvazquez310/status/2109679499906285836  
**Author:** Carlos (@cvazquez310) · posted 2026-10-12 (UTC) · 161 likes, 5 replies  

## What it is
@cvazquez310 advises not logging trials cancelled within 15 minutes as conversion events, because Meta's algorithm optimises for the events you send and will find more people who start and immediately cancel. His point: only send events you actually want more of.

## Thread highlights
- No notable replies came back from X search for this thread.

## Implications
- Applies to any subscription mobile game running paid acquisition.
- **Experiment:** delay trial-start events by 15+ minutes (or send only non-cancelled ones) on one ad set and compare trial-to-paid conversion after two weeks.
