## What it is
Ideogram launched 4.5, pitched as its most precise edit model: repeated edits avoid the usual artifacts, pixel shifts and colour drift, so multi-turn editing is usable. Live in Ideogram, the API and launch partners, with open weights promised 'soon'. Ideogram has not published numeric drift figures; a reply asked for them.

## Implications
- Concept art and UI work needs many small edits; a model that leaves untouched regions intact makes iterating on one key image viable.
- Promised open weights would allow local use alongside ComfyUI.
- **Experiment:** apply 10 consecutive edits to one image in 4.5 and a competitor, then diff the untouched region to measure drift.
