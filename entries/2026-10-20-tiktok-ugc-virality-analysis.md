# Analysing 4.5M TikToks: viral UGC isn't luck, and it isn't the format

**X post:** https://x.com/christianyun/status/2112588136597631304  
**Author:** christianyun (@christianyun) · posted 2026-10-20 (UTC) · 1,445 likes, 32 replies  

## What it is
An engineer at Lightreel (a UGC-insights company, per his bio) shares a video visualising 4,512,189 TikToks to test whether user-generated content going viral is luck. The post's claim is that it isn't, and that format isn't the deciding factor either. The detailed findings are in the video, which wasn't reviewed here. From replies, the analysis defines a breakout as about 10x a creator's usual views and compares breakout rates by creator, and an example contrasts a storytelling video with a plain greeting video. The author also links a list of recent 30-day trends in a reply.

## Implications
- **Mobile game marketing:** short-video UGC is a main channel for indie mobile games; the takeaway hinted at (story and creator quality over format templates) argues against copying trending formats blindly.
- **Method:** replies raise fair statistical objections, so treat the conclusion as a hypothesis.
- **Experiment:** post the same gameplay clip twice, once with a short story hook and once as a plain montage, across a few weeks, and compare view-through and breakout rate relative to your account's baseline.
