@He1s_Sammy lists ten open-source repos covering a content pipeline: OpenCreator (a creator workspace for Codex), social-media research skills for Claude Code/Codex, Local Deep Research, FLUX.2 (Klein 4B weights under Apache), Kokoro TTS, ACE-Step 1.5 music, Short Video Factory, an AI YouTube Shorts generator, Podcastfy, and a LangChain social media agent with draft review and scheduling.

## Implications
- Covers devlog and trailer production for small game teams: script, voice-over (Kokoro), music (ACE-Step), short-form cuts.
- Check each license before using generated assets commercially; only FLUX.2 Klein's license is stated in the post.
- **Experiment:** cut a 30-second devlog short from a long gameplay recording with the Shorts generator, add a Kokoro voice-over, and compare time spent with a manual edit.
