## What it is
@liambraus (Spanish) shares image-blaster, a GitHub repo that turns one photo into an explorable 3D world in ~5 minutes: meshes with physics, a background splat and ambient audio. Replies (via @grok) note the video and text are a near-exact repost of a May 2026 World Labs/image-blaster demo, so this isn't new.

## Implications
- Photo-to-world can give fast explorable environment prototypes before committing art time; splats aren't mobile-friendly, meshes are the useful part.
- **Experiment:** run one reference photo through it and check whether the mesh output fits mobile budgets in Unity.
