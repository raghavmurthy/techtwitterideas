## What it is
@0xGky (Chinese) highlights OpenDataLoader, a free PDF parser that converts ~100 pages/s to Markdown on CPU only, no GPU, cloud or API key. The post claims it tops benchmarks: docling ~15x slower, marker needs a GPU and is far slower, pymupdf4llm fast but weak on tables (0.40).
