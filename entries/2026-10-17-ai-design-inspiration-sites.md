## Implications
- A DESIGN.md handed to an agent is the design-system equivalent of AGENTS.md: useful for keeping AI-built game UI and store pages consistent.
- **Experiment:** write a DESIGN.md for a game's UI (palette, type, spacing, motion rules) using a getdesign.ai example as a template, and compare agent-built screens with and without it.
