# The Little Prince as a 3D story game in 16 days with Opus, Tripo and Blender

**X post:** https://x.com/Saccc_c/status/2109962425512734739  
**Author:** Sac (@Saccc_c) · posted 2026-10-13 (UTC) · 16693 likes, 1383 replies  

## What it is
@Saccc_c (in Chinese) says he spent 16 days using Opus 5.5 to turn The Little Prince into a 3D narrative game, with Tripo and Blender used to make each planet an explorable 3D scene; it is playable at littleprince.dev, and he plans to open-source the game and a detailed workflow. @BlendiByl, sharing it, called it a beautiful game and a sign of where games are headed given one person built it in 16 days. Replies praise the art while noting less fluid animation and an effects bug.

## Thread highlights
> **@BlendiByl:** such a beautiful game, truly. The fact that one person did this in 16 days should tell you everything about where games are headed

## Implications
- Concrete benchmark for a solo, agent-assisted 3D story game: Tripo for assets, Blender for cleanup, Opus for code and integration.
- **Experiment:** when the workflow is published, reproduce one planet scene end to end and log time per stage (generation, Blender cleanup, engine integration).
