# Apple Search Ads playbook for indie apps

**Author:** Frederick James (@frederickjames) · posted 2026-10-13 (UTC) · 354 likes, 39 replies  

## What it is
@frederickjames says he reached $2.5k/month in ~30 days with Apple Search Ads, starting from Apple's free $100 credit. His steps: exact-match keywords only, competitor names as keywords, campaigns split by territory with country-specific bids, start at Apple's recommended cost per tap and ~$25/day, wait two days, raise bids if there's no spend, have a strong store page and use new store assets, export data and have Claude find winners, then scale them. Caveats: it fails in saturated niches and in categories like finance where big companies bid high.

## Implications
- Competitor-name keywords suit niche mobile games where a larger title drives searches.
- **Experiment:** run a $25/day exact-match campaign on two competitor names for a game for a week and have an agent analyse the export.
