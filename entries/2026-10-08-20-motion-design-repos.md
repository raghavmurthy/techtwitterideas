## What it is
@N01ennn lists 20 GitHub repos for agent-made motion graphics: claude-motion (checks its own frames, sound in code), claude-motion-design (HTML + Playwright + ffmpeg), motion-graphics skills, Remotion skills, plus the engines HyperFrames, Remotion and GSAP. The 'Netflix engineers' framing isn't supported in the post. Replies say one HTML file + Playwright + ffmpeg is enough for simple work and ask for real run logs.

## Implications
- Picking one stack (HyperFrames or Remotion) is more useful than collecting all 20.
- **Experiment:** make a 10 s logo reveal with claude-motion and compare with HyperFrames.
