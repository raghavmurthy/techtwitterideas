## What it is
@Tu7uruu: Neuphonic released NeuDecide, a 43 MB audio-first decision model that maps speech straight to tool calls without transcription, 46 ms on an M3 MacBook, fully offline including mobile and Raspberry Pi, 75.5% tool accuracy on SLURP.
