# Claude Meetings: an assistant that builds docs and whiteboards during the video call

**Source:** the post and its 63-second demo video (the video was not transcribed here)

## What it is
Anthropic's official Claude account announced Claude Meetings: you add Claude to a team video call, and it builds docs, whiteboards and other artifacts with you while people are still talking, rather than producing notes afterwards. It is starting to roll out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans.

The post itself gives no further details. Replies add some, unverified here: one says it leaves when an outside guest joins and stays out of guest calls by default; another says notes and transcripts are free during the beta, docs and whiteboards built in a call are free until 10 February 2027, and answers that use connected tools count toward plan usage. Check Anthropic's own documentation before relying on either.

## Implications
- **From notes to artifacts:** the shift is that the meeting output is a working document (a spec, a diagram, a task list) rather than a summary. For small teams, a design review can end with a draft design doc already written.
- **Game design sessions:** level-design and feature-planning calls are a good fit: a live whiteboard of a level flow or an economy loop, and a draft spec that an agent can later turn into tickets or code.
- **Privacy defaults matter:** if the guest-call behaviour reported in the replies holds, that is a sensible pattern for any in-call agent, especially with playtesters or outside partners on the call.
- **Watch usage cost:** if connected-tool answers count toward plan usage, long calls could eat into limits.
- **Experiment:** if you have a Team or Enterprise plan, run one 30-minute feature-planning call with Claude Meetings and one without. A week later, check whether the in-call artifact was actually reused (edited, linked or turned into tasks) compared with notes written by hand.
