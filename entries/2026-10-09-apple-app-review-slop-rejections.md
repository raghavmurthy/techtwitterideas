# Avoiding Apple rejections for 'spam/slop' apps

**X post:** https://x.com/MaxHirsch13/status/2108623104104976554  
**Author:** max (@MaxHirsch13) · posted 2026-10-09 (UTC) · 453 likes, 28 replies  

## What it is
@MaxHirsch13 says Apple is rejecting more low-effort apps and advises: have more than one feature, use a design that isn't a clone, and give proof in the review notes that the app was built from scratch. He shared his Figma file and commit history.

## Implications
- Small AI-assisted mobile games risk the same rejection; keeping design files and commit history ready is cheap insurance.
- **Experiment:** before the next iOS submission, prepare a short review note with design-file screenshots and a commit timeline, and track whether review time changes.
