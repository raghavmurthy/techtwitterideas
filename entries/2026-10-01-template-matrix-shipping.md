## What it is
@intentplex (in Chinese) describes teams that ship several 100k-line products a day by reskinning a large template library (chat, video generation, dashboards, mini-games, plus login/payments/subscriptions/SEO). The practice is called 马甲包 / 'matrix play'; mobile games do the same via reskins. The author argues the moat is the template library, ad-buying experience and speed of reading data, not code.

## Implications
- For indie studios: login, IAP, subscriptions and analytics are worth packaging as a reusable engine template so new prototypes are reskins, not rebuilds.
- **Experiment:** list every non-gameplay system in a current project and mark which could be extracted into a reusable Unity package.
