# How Block Blast! designs losses around ads

**X post:** https://x.com/blakeir/status/2110755359409516772  
**Author:** Blake Robbins (@blakeir) · posted 2026-10-15 (UTC) · 14213 likes, 110 replies  

## What it is
Blake Robbins (@blakeir), co-host of the Gamecraft podcast and formerly at Benchmark and Ludlow Ventures per his bio, shares what he calls the best breakdown of Block Blast!, the most downloaded mobile game last year: it intentionally makes players lose to optimise which ads they see. The breakdown is a video by @TheDonAyo, part of a series where he uses AI to reverse-engineer mobile games (another covers Subway Surfers). The video content wasn't read here.

## Implications
- Shows how much monetisation logic hides inside 'simple' casual games, and that AI-assisted teardown of competitors is now practical.
- Designing losses for ad yield is a player-trust trade-off worth deciding on deliberately.
- **Experiment:** use an agent to analyse a top casual game's session flow from recorded play and list every point where difficulty or ads change.
