## What it is
Tanishq, who is building an agentic operating system at Cashew Labs per his X bio, quotes his own earlier post ("new prompt cheat code found", https://x.com/tanishqk/status/2115651175467106518) and adds that it gives "actually unbelievable results for planning". The earlier post's screenshot shows a chat prompt asking the model to first help check whether his logic and recommendations hold up, ending with the phrase "exercise abstract thinking." Asked whether this is just "be creative", he says it is less about creativity and more about abstract reasoning. No examples, comparisons or measurements are given, so this is one person's impression.

## Implications
- **Cheap to test, easy to fool yourself:** a one-line suffix costs nothing, but impressions of "better plans" are hard to judge without side-by-side outputs.
- **Pairs with plan-first workflows:** asking the model to check the logic first and reason about structure before steps fits planning work like feature breakdowns or asset pipeline design.
- **Experiment:** take three real planning tasks (for example, a Blender-to-Unity export pipeline change, a save-system refactor and a level-streaming plan), run each with and without the suffix in fresh sessions, and have a second model rate the plans blind for missing steps and wrong assumptions.
